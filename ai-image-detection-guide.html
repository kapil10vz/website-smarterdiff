AI Image Detection Guide

The AI-vs-real score is a heuristic signal, not a final determination. Treat it as one part of your review process.

Recommended usage

  • Run a score, then review metadata and context.
  • Compare source and reposted versions if available.
  • Document uncertainty in your notes.

When confidence drops

Heuristics become less stable on heavily edited, re-compressed, cropped, or screenshot-derived images.
