Compare Excel Files

Use this page when you need to inspect spreadsheet revisions and identify changed cells quickly.

Step-by-step

  1. Go to the Excel tab in SmarterDiff.
  2. Upload workbook A and workbook B.
  3. Select the sheet you want to review.
  4. Check highlighted cells and note before/after values.

Good review habits

Review formula-driven sheets first, then summary sheets. If your workbook has many tabs, compare one sheet at a time and keep a short change log.
