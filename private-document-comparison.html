Private Document Comparison

This checklist helps reduce risk when reviewing legal, financial, or internal documents.

Before you compare

  • Use a trusted device and updated browser.
  • Close unrelated tabs and screen-sharing sessions.
  • Confirm you are on smarterdiff.com.

During review

  • Load only the documents needed for the current decision.
  • Capture only necessary excerpts in notes.
  • If needed, run comparisons in a private browsing session.

After review

  • Close the tab to clear in-memory data.
  • Store your review notes in your approved system.
  • Share findings, not raw source documents, unless required.
